Integrated Agricultural Unit: Agriculture, Livestock and Fisheries

Supporting sustainable livelihoods and economic opportunities

Agriculture, livestock, and fisheries are important sources of income for many communities in Bangladesh. In the Chattogram Hill Tracts, these sectors have significant potential to support livelihoods and improve economic opportunities. The Integrated Development Foundation (IDF) has been focusing on these areas since its inception.

IDF’s Integrated Agriculture Program

IDF started its integrated agriculture activities in 1996 with the assistance of Sida. Later, HKI supported activities such as home gardening, nutrition, fruit gardening, and local poultry raising. Training on nutrition management was also included to help communities improve their food and nutritional practices.

Agriculture and Home Gardening

Home gardening can help families produce fresh vegetables and fruits for household consumption. It can also create opportunities to generate additional income by selling surplus agricultural products. These activities encourage families to make better use of available land and resources.

🌱 Sustainable Agriculture: Home gardening and agricultural activities can improve food security, nutrition, and household income.

Livestock and Fisheries

Livestock and fisheries are also important parts of the program. These sectors can contribute to household income, employment, food security, and nutrition. IDF later established a separate Agriculture, Livestock and Fisheries Unit to continue and strengthen activities in these areas.

Training and Modern Technology

Since 2014, PKSF has been providing technical and financial support to the program. This support includes training, introducing new technologies, and improving the marketing of agricultural products. These initiatives can help producers develop their skills and improve their access to markets.

Supporting Sustainable Livelihoods

The Integrated Agricultural Unit focuses on more than agricultural production. By combining agriculture, livestock, fisheries, training, technology, and marketing support, the program aims to create better livelihood opportunities for communities in underserved areas.

Conclusion

IDF’s Integrated Agricultural Unit is an important initiative for strengthening agriculture, livestock, and fisheries. Through training, technology, and market-oriented support, the program contributes to improved livelihoods and economic opportunities for local communities.
